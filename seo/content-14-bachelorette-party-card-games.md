# Best Bachelorette Party Card Games (For When Everyone Needs to Sit Down for a Minute)

**Short answer:** Most bachelorette party game lists are all activities
&mdash; sashes, dares, scavenger hunts, a cucumber-passing game you'll
never speak of again. Good, but none of it works at 11pm when everyone's
feet hurt and the group just wants to sit down without the night
stalling out. That's the gap card games fill, and **Eat Your Neighbor**
is built specifically for that lull: 20&ndash;30 minutes, zero setup,
dark comedy energy that matches the rest of the night.

Bachelorette parties run on momentum, and most of the planning goes
into the loud, physical stuff. The card game moment almost always gets
an afterthought pick &mdash; usually whatever's already in someone's
bag. Here's a shortlist worth actually planning for.

## 1. Eat Your Neighbor &mdash; for the "let's sit down but keep the energy" moment

Every player raises a family of small creatures called Nugs, and the
whole table tries to get someone else's family eaten, buried, or run
over first. It's fast (20&ndash;30 minutes), teaches in under 5, and
keeps the same "everyone's roasting each other" energy as the rest of
the night &mdash; just from a chair instead of a dance floor. 2&ndash;6
players, so it works whether it's the whole party or a smaller group
that's peeled off to the porch.
[See sample cards &rarr;](https://eatyourneighbor.com/#cards)

## 2. Cards Against Humanity &mdash; the reliable default

Everyone's played it, most people already have an app or a physical
deck. Scales up to a big group easily, though the humor comes from the
prompt cards rather than anything players are actually doing to each
other.

## 3. Kings Cup &mdash; if the group wants the cards to double as a drinking game

Just a standard deck and a cup in the middle, with an action assigned
to each card. Works for a big circle and doesn't require buying
anything extra, though it needs someone to actually know or look up
the card rules.

## 4. Never Have I Ever &mdash; zero materials required

Not technically a card game, but it belongs on this list because it's
the one every bachelorette party ends up playing anyway, card deck or
not. Good icebreaker earlier in the night; loses steam if it's the only
game played.

## 5. Bride-Themed Trivia (Mr. and Mrs. quiz, "who knows the bride best")

Low effort, high payoff &mdash; write a set of questions about the
bride ahead of time and let the group compete to know her best. Best
used as a structured, planned moment rather than a spontaneous one,
since it needs prep beforehand.

## Which one should you actually bring?

- **Want something to sit down to that doesn't kill the energy?** Eat Your Neighbor.
- **Want the safest, most universally known option?** Cards Against Humanity.
- **Want the cards to double as a drinking game?** Kings Cup.
- **Want a planned, sentimental moment centered on the bride?** Trivia.
