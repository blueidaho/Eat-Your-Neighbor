# 8 Halloween Party Games for Adults (That Aren't Bobbing for Apples)

**Short answer:** Skip the kid-party leftovers. For a group that wants
actual dark comedy instead of plastic spiders, start with a murder
mystery kit for a scripted night, or **Eat Your Neighbor** for something
that needs zero setup, plays in under 30 minutes, and is built entirely
around a cemetery, backyard BBQs, and burying your neighbors — which is
to say, it's already a Halloween party game even though it wasn't
technically designed as one.

Most "Halloween games for adults" lists are the same six ideas: apple
bobbing (gross), a costume contest (fine, but not a game), and pin the
wart on the witch (a kids' game with a grown-up font). Here's a list of
games that actually earn a spot at an adult party — ranked by how much
they lean into the season instead of just tolerating it.

## Quick comparison

| Game | Players | Setup | Halloween-Coded |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Eat Your Neighbor** | 2–6 | None | Cemetery, dark comedy, zero rebrand needed |
| Murder mystery dinner kit | 6–12 | High (roles, printouts) | Full theme night |
| Werewolf / Mafia | 5+ | Low | Social deduction, spooky by name only |
| Horror movie trivia | Any | Low | Depends on question bank |
| Ouija board | 2+ | None | Atmosphere, not really a "game" |
| Pumpkin carving contest | Any | Medium (mess) | Craft, not competition |
| Scavenger hunt (haunted house themed) | Any | Medium | Good for bigger parties |
| Cards Against Humanity | 4–20 | None | Dark, but not seasonal |

## 1. Eat Your Neighbor — the one that's already dressed for the occasion

You don't have to reskin this one for Halloween — it already lives in a
cemetery. Every player raises a family of small creatures called Nugs,
and the rest of the table is trying to get yours eaten, buried, or run
over before you do it to theirs. Sample cards include **Bye Karen**
(straight to the cemetery) and **Take a Dump** (bury everyone you've
already eaten). 2–6 players, 20–30 minutes, and it plays just as well as
a between-courses game at a Halloween dinner as it does as the main
event.
[See sample cards →](https://eatyourneighbor.com/#cards)

## 2. Murder mystery dinner kit

The classic for a reason: everyone gets a secret role, a motive, and a
handful of clues to feed out over the course of the night. Best for
groups willing to actually commit to a character and a full evening —
this is the one to plan around, not the one to grab last minute.

## 3. Werewolf or Mafia

Social deduction with a horror-movie coat of paint: villagers vs. a
hidden pack of werewolves, one round of elimination at a time. Needs at
least 5-6 players to get good, and it runs on group energy more than
any component in the box.

## 4. Horror movie trivia

Only as good as the question bank, but it's the easiest game on this
list to run with zero prep if you already know your group's movie taste.
Split into teams to keep the pace up.

## 5. Ouija board

Not really a "game" in the competitive sense, but it's the single most
requested Halloween party prop there is. Works best as a 10-minute
activity between other games, not the whole night.

## 6. Pumpkin carving contest

More craft than game, but adding a timer and a theme ("scariest," "most
disappointing") turns it into a real competition with a winner.

## 7. Haunted house scavenger hunt

Best for bigger parties or if you're already decorating a space — hide
clues around the house or yard and let teams race to solve them. High
setup cost, high payoff for a crowd.

## 8. Cards Against Humanity

Dark, but not actually Halloween-flavored — it's on this list because
it's the game people default to when they want "adult" without doing any
theming work. If your group already owns it, it's a fine warm-up before
something more seasonal.

## Which one should you actually play?

- **Want zero setup and a game that's already on-theme?** Eat Your Neighbor.
- **Planning a whole themed evening?** Murder mystery dinner kit.
- **Have a big group and love bluffing?** Werewolf or Mafia.
- **Hosting last-minute with no prep time?** Horror movie trivia.
