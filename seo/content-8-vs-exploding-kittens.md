# Eat Your Neighbor vs. Exploding Kittens: Which Should You Buy?

**Short answer:** Buy Exploding Kittens if you want a lighter, family-safe
game where the threat comes from a shared shuffled deck. Buy **Eat Your
Neighbor** if you've already got Exploding Kittens and want a darker,
more direct game where you can target a specific opponent by name instead
of just hoping you don't draw the wrong card.

They're close cousins mechanically — fast rounds, card-based sabotage, a
rulebook you can explain in under five minutes — but the actual *feeling*
at the table is different enough that "which one should I buy" has a real
answer depending on your group.

## The core difference

**Exploding Kittens** is a shared-threat game. The danger (the exploding
kitten card) sits in a deck everyone draws from. Nobody chose to target
you specifically — you just drew badly. 2–5 players.

**Eat Your Neighbor** is a direct-threat game. Cards like **Bye Karen**
and **Road Kill** let a specific player choose to come after your specific
"family" of Nugs. When it happens, you know exactly who did it and why.
2–6 players, about 20–30 minutes.

That one design choice changes the social temperature of the whole game.
Exploding Kittens creates tension through randomness. Eat Your Neighbor
creates tension through personal accountability — the friend across the
table just did that to you, on purpose, and everyone saw it happen.

## Side-by-side

| | Exploding Kittens | Eat Your Neighbor |
|---|---|---|
| Players | 2–5 | 2–6 |
| Play time | ~15 minutes | 20–30 minutes |
| Threat type | Shared deck (random) | Direct, player-targeted |
| Tone | Family-friendly chaos | Dark comedy, adult-leaning |
| Components | Cards only | Board, dice, cards |

## Which one should you actually buy?

- **New to this style of game entirely?** Start with Exploding Kittens —
  it's the gentler on-ramp.
- **Already own Exploding Kittens and want more?** Eat Your Neighbor is
  built for exactly that upgrade.
- **Want family-safe for a wide age range?** Exploding Kittens.
- **Want your adult friend group to actually gasp out loud?** Eat Your
  Neighbor.

Most groups end up wanting both — they solve slightly different nights.
