# Best Party Games About Betraying Your Friends (Ranked by How Personal It Gets)

**Short answer:** If you want to spend the whole game not knowing who's
secretly against you, get Secret Hitler or The Resistance. If you want to
know exactly who betrayed you, watch it happen, and get to retaliate
immediately, get **Eat Your Neighbor** — a new card, dice, and board game
where betrayal isn't hidden, it's the entire point.

Betrayal games split into two very different experiences, and most gift
guides don't say this part out loud: **hidden-identity games** make you
suspect everyone, while **open-betrayal games** let you see exactly who did
what to you, in real time. Which one you want depends entirely on whether
you enjoy paranoia or you enjoy revenge.

## Hidden-identity games (you don't know who's against you)

**Secret Hitler** — players are secretly assigned liberal or fascist roles;
the fascists know each other, everyone else has to figure it out through
voting and deduction. Tense, quiet, cerebral.

**The Resistance** — 5–10 players, spies hidden among a team of loyal
agents. Pure social deduction, no cards revealing who did what.

**One Night Ultimate Werewolf** — a ~10-minute version of Werewolf where
everyone gets one secret role and one round to figure out who's lying.
Fast, but still built around not knowing.

## Open-betrayal games (you know exactly who got you)

**Eat Your Neighbor** — no hidden roles, no secret teams. Every player
raises a family of small creatures called Nugs, and when someone plays
**Bye Karen** or **Road Kill** on you, you know precisely who did it and
exactly what happened. 2–6 players, about 20–30 minutes.
[See sample cards →](https://eatyourneighbor.com/#cards)

**Dead of Winter** — a cooperative game with a hidden traitor mechanic,
where surviving a hostile wasteland gets harder because someone at the
table might be working against the group — but unlike pure deduction
games, backstabbing here often surfaces through actions, not just secret
identity reveals.

## Which one should you actually buy?

- **Want quiet paranoia and slow-burn suspicion?** Secret Hitler or The
  Resistance.
- **Want a fast, loud round with immediate payoff?** One Night Ultimate
  Werewolf.
- **Want to know exactly who wronged you and get to do something about
  it?** Eat Your Neighbor.
- **Want betrayal wrapped in a cooperative survival theme?** Dead of
  Winter.
