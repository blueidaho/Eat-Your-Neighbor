# Keyword Research — Eat Your Neighbor

Business: Eat Your Neighbor (card/dice/board game). ICP: Exploding Kittens fans —
people who want fast, silly, dark-humor party games. Site: eatyourneighbor.com
(currently a pre-launch Kickstarter waitlist page; no reviews/press/branded
search volume exist yet).

Methodology note: no DataForSEO/Ahrefs connection available in this session, so
"Traffic" below is a qualitative tier (High/Med/Low) inferred from how many
independent listicle domains currently compete for the term, how frequently the
term is refreshed year-over-year, and adjacent evidence (Kickstarter activity,
retailer category pages, gift-guide density). Treat these as directional, not
exact volumes — verify with a real keyword tool (Ahrefs/Semrush/DataForSEO)
before committing paid budget.

## Cluster 1 — "Games like Exploding Kittens" (discovery / comparison)
The single richest opportunity: at least 8 independent listicle sites
(Bustle, GigaBrain, EditionCards, Find Game Like, TheGamer, GameNightGods,
Very Special Games, Great Board Games Blog, Don't Eat the Meeples) actively
compete for this cluster and refresh it annually — confirms durable, real
demand, and **none currently mention Eat Your Neighbor**, since none of them
predate our existence. This is the highest-leverage, most winnable cluster.

| exploding kittens alternatives | High | Med-High | ⭐ Top priority |

## Cluster 2 — Dark humor / NSFW party card games

(coopboardgames.com, thegamersmag.com, lost-boy-entertainment.com), meaning
domain authority bar to compete is low. This cluster is Eat Your Neighbor's
most authentic positioning (it out-darks Exploding Kittens directly), so it's
both winnable AND on-brand.

## Cluster 4 — Crowdfunding / new-release discovery
Real, active demand (BoardGameGeek geeklists, Kickstarter's own discover pages,
Bitewing/SinfulShadows "most anticipated" roundups) from a highly relevant,
high-intent audience: people who specifically fund and buy new tabletop games
early. This is the fastest path to actual backers, not just generic traffic.

|---|---|---|---|

## Cluster 6 — Thematic / novelty angle (long-tail, low competition, on-brand)
Nobody owns this exact framing yet — low search volume individually, but
cheap to rank for and reinforces the brand's specific hook (cannibalism +
suburbia + betrayal), which also makes these pages strong candidates for
AI-answer citation since there's no dominant existing source.

## Top priorities selected for content (Step 3)
1. **"Games like Exploding Kittens"** (Cluster 1) — highest realistic traffic
   ceiling, proven durable demand, zero current mention of us, format =
   listicle that lists Eat Your Neighbor prominently among real alternatives.
2. **"Dark humor card games for adults"** (Cluster 2) — second-highest
   traffic, weakest competing domain authority, most authentic to the brand.
3. **"New party card games on Kickstarter 2026"** (Cluster 4) — lower raw
   traffic than #1/#2 but the highest-intent audience (people who actively
   fund new games), and doubles as direct Kickstarter-launch amplification.
